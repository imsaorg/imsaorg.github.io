What we do, and what why it matters

We arrange guidance sessions, teach one-another, go one charity drives, and make the best of our medical journey.


We are a non-profit initiative taken by Muslim medicos from all over the Telangana with an aim to create a unified platform for muslim medical students, working towards self-improvement and upliftment of the soceity under the divine light and guidance of Islam.

A word from our founders

What began as a journey I never really planned became one of the most meaningful parts of my life ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّٰه
Allah brought me into IMSA with a vision to please Him, strengthen our Iman and use our profession to serve the community. Along the way IMSA became a journey of personal growth for me. Through teamwork, social service and responsibilities I learnt to communicate better, organise myself, lead and recognise my own shortcomings. It also strengthened me academically and gave me opportunities to help fellow medicos grow in their academics, skills and confidence. More importantly it taught me to carry compassion, ethics and a sense of responsibility into my profession. To everyone who becomes part of IMSA, Don't lose this opportunity, Serve wholeheartedly, uplift others and leave the place better than you found it The greatest mark of leadership is not how far you go but how far others go because of you. I hope IMSA continues to be a place where people grow, serve and inspire one another for the sake of Allah ان شاء الله

Fariha di
