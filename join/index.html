    Initiative of Medicos for Service and Academics (IMSA) is a platform that brings together medical students across Telangana to grow through service, academics, leadership, and meaningful connections.

    We believe that being a medico is not just about learning medicine, but also about developing compassion, strengthening our faith, serving humanity, and making a positive difference in society.

    Through academic initiatives, community outreach, skill-building sessions, mentorship, and collaborative projects, IMSA provides opportunities for students to learn, lead, and serve beyond the classroom.

    Join IMSA and become part of a community that learns together, serves together, and grows together.
