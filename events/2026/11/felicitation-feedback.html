    2026 Batch Felicitation, a Grand Success

    3 minute read

    Alhamdulillahi Rabbil Aalameen. All praise be to the Lord of the Worlds.

    We tried our best within our finite means and effort to deliver a useful and impacting session to the newly joined batch of 2026 MBBS students. It was a day attended by several hundreds of people, much more than what we expected.

    The speeches, the seminars, the panel discussions… They were very well-received by the audience, who exhibited no signs of disinterest even after 5 hours of the event.

    This is what a volunteer had to say:

    Jazakallah Khair, everyone! The event was truly successful because of volunteers like you who wholeheartedly came forward . It was genuinely a wonderful experience working with each one of you. Despite your busy study schedules, you all took out time and put in so much effort, and that truly makes me proud of all of us. Your efforts are deeply appreciated. 👏🏻

    Your dedication, enthusiasm were truly remarkable and beyond what words can express. MashaAllah! 🌟

    May Allah (Subhaanawataala) accept all your efforts, place immense barakah in your work, and reward each one of you abundantly for being a means of guidance and benefit to others. Ameen. ✨

    This sentiment was shared by several others, who said

    First of all, الحمدالله, all praise is to Allah for giving us all this huge opportunity to help and guide the new Muslim Medicos and create a strong network and companionship. The execution was absolutely flawless, despite the challenges, despite the shortage of time and the excess of crowd, everything was handled amazingly. It is all because of each and everyone’s effort here to make this happen.

    And the result? Insane! The response and feedback even more الحمدالله

    May Allah accept everyone’s efforts and time spent in making this happen and reward us abundantly. Ameen.

    Several members of the audience found the event immensely helpful, and found the stalls wonderful. The Bone stall. The games. The speciality stall. The Newsletter.

    The Newsletter?! Yes, the IMSA newsletter! The Mishkat.

    I would behard-pressed for words to describe how the students found the Mishkat (lantern) newsletter. the loved the riddles in it. Its design, its typeface, its art was well-appreciated that day.

    May God abundantly reward all the members involved in it.

    Coming back to the D-Day. It was attended by several doctors, and several parents of the students. They found it fantastic, and were keen on attending future events.

    Such a positive note to end this day. May God abundantly reward all those involved.
